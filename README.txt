SOL BY SOL
Can you keep a Mars base alive for 10 sols?

HOW TO START
1. Unzip the whole folder first. Do not open the game from inside the zip.
2. Open the SolbySol folder.
3. Double-click index.html. The game opens in your web browser.

You do not need the internet, an account or an install.
It works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

HOW TO PLAY
Press LAUNCH. Pip the robot teaches you while you play the first turns.
If you want the short version:
- Each turn starts with an event. Read it first.
- Click the cards you want to use this turn. Pick as many as your power allows.
- Picked the wrong card? Press the minus button on it.
- Press "End turn". Your cards are played one by one.
- Watch your three meters: Health, Power and Radiation.
- On sol 9 the Sun flares. Take shelter while the storm is strong.
- Explore and Hand crank are risky. Keep them for emergencies.

The solar storm in the game is real: it hit Mars in September 2017.

For the team: NOTES.md explains what each file does.
